---
title: Markdawn vs Apple Notes
description: Compare Markdawn and Apple Notes for personal capture, shared project structure, and portability.
---

Apple Notes is a low-friction personal notes app for people who want to capture information quickly across Apple devices. Markdawn is a better candidate when information needs shared project structure, broader access, and a markdown-centered content layer.

| Decision Area | Markdawn | Apple Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main job | Shared project knowledge | Personal capture and quick notes |
| Device experience | Browser, CLI, and self-hosted server | Apple device ecosystem |
| Organization | Pages, folders, and links | Folders, tags, and note search |
| Portability | Markdown and Obsidian imports | Export requires testing |

Choose Apple Notes for effortless personal capture, mobile convenience, sketches, and Apple workflows. Choose Markdawn when notes need to become shared project context.

## What To Test Before Switching

Test permissions, device requirements, editing behavior, shared notes, and export cleanup with real notes.

## Verdict

Choose Apple Notes for personal capture. Choose Markdawn when notes need to become shared project context.
