---
title: Markdawn vs Craft
description: Compare Markdawn and Craft for polished documents, markdown portability, and infrastructure control.
---

Craft is a polished document and notes application for people who care about writing quality, visual presentation, and organized documents. Markdawn is a stronger candidate when markdown portability, self-hosting, and tool access are central requirements.

| Decision Area | Markdawn | Craft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Main job | Shared project knowledge | Polished notes and documents |
| Content model | Markdown pages, folders, and links | Rich document and block experience |
| Collaboration | Shared pages and editing | Collaborative documents and sharing |
| Hosting | Hosted or self-hosted | Managed application |

Choose Craft for polished writing and visual presentation. Choose Markdawn for a simpler, inspectable content model that people and tools can use together.

## What To Test Before Switching

Export representative pages and check links, images, tables, nested content, and formatting.

## Verdict

Choose Craft for polished documents. Choose Markdawn for durable, inspectable project knowledge.
